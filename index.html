Ingredients

  • 2 medium Cucumber
  • Half TSP Salt
  • 2 TSP Sesame Seed Oil
  • 2 tablespoons Rice Vinegar
  • 6 Basil Leaves
  • Half TSP Red Chili Flakes
  • 2 TSP Sesame Seeds

Directions

  1. Peel the cucumbers
  2. Cut them into quarters by length
  3. Discard bitter seeds
  4. Cut the cucumbers into half-inch thick pieces
  5. Place the cucumbers into a serving bowl. Sprinkle them with salt, sesame oil, rice vinegar, basil, and chili flakes.
